# Yanbu33 Meteor Rain (Minecraft 1.21.1 + Fabric)
TNT meteor rain: 120 primed TNT entities distributed around your exact position within a 100-block radius. Lucky meteor rain: 72 falling Lucky Blocks within 20 blocks of you, exactly 10 seconds before TNT. Starts at 60-second intervals, shortens by 10 seconds every 5 minutes to a minimum of 30 seconds. Runs per player.
Commands for operators: `/meteors start`, `/meteors stop`, `/meteors reset`, `/meteors status`.
Requires Minecraft Java 1.21.1, Fabric Loader, and Fabric API. Keep backup worlds: this intentionally creates many explosions and may cause lag.
To download a compiled mod, open the repository **Actions** tab, choose the **Build Meteor Rain JAR** workflow, open a successful run, and download the **meteor-rain-jar** artifact ZIP; extract the .jar and copy it into your Modrinth instance's mods folder.
